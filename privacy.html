Privacy Policy

Seraphina Studio respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information submitted through our website.

Information We Collect

When you submit a booking enquiry, we collect the information you provide: name, email address, WhatsApp number, preferred destination, service details, preferred date, and any additional requirements you share.

How We Use Information

We use enquiry information solely to respond to your request, confirm availability, discuss packages, and arrange bookings. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

WhatsApp

When you contact us via WhatsApp, your message is subject to WhatsApp’s own privacy practices. We use the conversation only for the purpose of your enquiry.

Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as necessary to manage your booking and for reasonable administrative purposes.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, email us at theseraphinastudio@gmail.com.
