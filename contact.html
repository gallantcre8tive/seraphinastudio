How We Handle Enquiries

  1. You submit an enquiry via form or WhatsApp.
  2. We review your requested service, destination, and date.
  3. We check local availability of professionals, equipment, and (if needed) dress rental.
  4. We confirm final package details and pricing with you.
  5. Booking is confirmed only after arrangements and terms are agreed.
We do not automatically confirm bookings or invent availability. For large projects, weddings, and commercial productions, a personalised quote will be provided.
